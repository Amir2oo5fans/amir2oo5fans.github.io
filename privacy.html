Privacy Policy

Undead Hours Tools · last updated November 13, 2026

Undead Hours Tools ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Undead Hours YouTube channel. This page explains what Google and YouTube data the app uses and how.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

Only after the channel owner signs in and gives permission on Google's consent screen, the app accesses data of the signed-in YouTube channel:

  • channel and video information (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy status, comments), through the youtube.force-ssl permission;
  • YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, subscribers gained), through the yt-analytics.readonly permission.

How the data is used

  • To show the channel owner how their own videos perform, and to plan better videos.
  • To update the owner's own videos when the owner asks for it.

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

The sign-in token is kept as a private secret in the owner's own workspace. Summaries of the analytics (for example, where viewers stop watching a video) are kept in the owner's private project files. Nothing is published.

Your control

You can remove the app's access at any time on your Google account's third-party access page (also at Google security settings). After that, the app can no longer read or change anything, and stored copies are deleted on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this site's GitHub page.
