Undead Hours Tools

A private helper for the Undead Hours YouTube channel.

What it does

Undead Hours Tools is used only by the owner of the Undead Hours channel. After the owner signs in with their own Google account and gives permission on Google's consent screen, it:

  • reads the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) so the owner can see how each video performs;
  • updates the channel's own videos when the owner asks for it: titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and comment replies.

It works only on the owner's own channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
